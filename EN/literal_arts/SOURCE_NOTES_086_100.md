# Potential source inconsistencies: chapters 086–100

These are observations made during translation, not a comprehensive independent fact-check. Chapters preserve the source's claims, examples, arguments and quotations; quotations are translated from the supplied Chinese, not represented as verified transcriptions of a particular English edition. References identify the extracted Chinese fragments under `/private/tmp/ignite-curiosity-liberal-sources/` (derived from the repository's Chinese book).

## Chapter 086: What Exactly Is Capitalism?

- The Manchester reconstruction uses named composite figures while saying every detail can be checked, but supplies no individual archival citations. The claim that a sixteen-year-old's12–14-hourdayin1842violated no contemporary law needs checking against Factory Act age/hour restrictions. The distribution section deliberately returns to Mary making tea despite identifying her as a textile worker.
- The five-component definition is a particular interpretive framework, not a universally agreed necessary-and-sufficient definition. Wage earners need not own no assets or have absolutely no alternative livelihood; enterprises need not be incorporated legal persons; household deposits can fund capital through financial intermediation. These categorical source statements remain unchanged.
- Earlier Venice/Genoa and other merchant societies are excluded from capitalism, while the later history explicitly calls Venice commercial capitalism. The text shifts criteria from dominant wage-labor production to a broader commercial category without resolving the tension. VOC-first-public-share and money-growth-faster-than-any-factory claims also merit historical qualification.
- Smith, Marx and Polanyi are simplified interpretive types. Smith's profit theory is not reducible to managerial wages/risk compensation; Marxian surplus value is not simply sales revenue minus worker wages, and critique via organizational labor needs the source theory's distinctions. Polanyi's account and the statement that Marx first asked profit's origin are broad historical claims, not fully documented here.
- The Smith same-trade passage's Chinese paraphrase can suggest merchants rarely meet at all, rather than meetings rarely ending without collusion. The English preserves the intended conditional force. Supplied Sima Qian, Smith and Marx passages are translated directly; no modern copyrighted English translation is reproduced.
- Milk-tea shares, labor alternatives, universal platform practices, education-involution analogy and claims of guaranteed immediate rider employment are illustrative generalizations, not independently verified economic data. The source's opposing arguments and qualifications are all retained.

## Chapter 087: Nations

- Passport history is sweeping: standardized interwar formats are distinguished insufficiently from older passports, travel controls and nationality documentation. The claim that a1920sformis younger than today's grandfather is dated rhetoric. Crossing Europe without papers was not universal, especially for marginalized populations and different periods/routes. Passport nationality and citizenship are not perfectly interchangeable in every jurisdiction; adoption/residence does not automatically confer citizenship everywhere.
- Alsace-Lorraine transfer is simplified as transfer to Prussia, and Lorraine was not ceded in its entirety. The date and uniform school-language order in the Daudet account need verification; fictional characters' feelings are not direct evidence of all Alsatians' preferences. Repeated categorical Alsatian choice overlooks contested, changing affiliations.
- Earlier national/ethnic identities and modern mass nationalism are contested theoretical distinctions, not settled all-or-nothing birthdays. Five-century dialect mutual intelligibility and uninterrupted two-millennia ethnic histories are broad illustrative claims. Four Swiss national languages are not uniformly official for every administrative purpose; national and ethnic identity need careful distinction.
- Sun's1924political context is described as needing to overthrow Qing, although the dynasty had already fallen. His1924nationalism lectures and earlier multiethnic revolutionary positions should not be conflated. The natural-five-forces versus voluntary-Renan contrast is an analytical simplification.
- Indonesia's1928Youth Pledge says upholding Indonesian as the language of unity, not necessarily denying other languages. The claim that Indonesia existed in no sense before1928contradicts the acknowledged earlier name and colonial framework. Malay is associated with Malay communities as well as trade; language choice was not literally nobody's ethnic language.
- Turkish script reform had implementation and generational processes, not an instantaneous erasure of all historical access. The colonial Gaulish-ancestor schoolbook example, French-language percentages and claimed universal political cause of standardization require source-specific qualification. Anderson's book definition is translated from the supplied Chinese, not a checked English-edition quotation.
- The text preserves contrasting political positions and authorial critiques. These notes do not resolve normative membership questions or offer current immigration/legal guidance.

## Chapter 088: Imperialism

- The Berlin conference scene includes detailed sensory reconstruction without an identified eyewitness citation. The General Act did not itself draw every later African border or directly partition all the interior; effective-occupation provisions, subsequent bilateral treaties, conquest and local agency need differentiation. The1875/1900control percentages and global1914land estimate depend on definitions and dates not supplied.
- The straight-African-versus-natural-European-border contrast is rhetorical: neither nature nor European territorial history is devoid of straight boundaries, and African lines also reflect local geography and negotiations. The source hints at Shanghai park signage without specifying what it said; the translation deliberately does not introduce a purported sign quotation.
- Direct Crown rule in India began in1858, not1876;1876relates to the imperial title. India also retained indirectly governed princely states, and Vietnam had differing colonial/protectorate arrangements. Congo Free State personal rule and Belgian colonial administration are distinct periods. The three-type tool is useful but simplified, as the source acknowledges.
- Sykes–Picot was one wartime arrangement, not the exact final blueprint of all present Middle Eastern borders. Ottoman debt administration, tariffs and sovereignty had multiple legal mechanisms. The phrase no occupation is a general model rather than a complete regional chronology.
- Hobson and Lenin have distinct economic arguments, not one identical excess-capital narrative. Technology, racism, economic incentives and rivalry are contested explanatory weights; saying any one was indispensable is stronger than the chapter's evidence establishes. Wallerstein's framework and Japan-as-periphery-to-core comparison are simplified interpretations.
- Quinine reference describes historical malaria control, not current medical guidance. Colonial famine/population-loss estimates remain source-qualified. The colonial inheritance discussion preserves both extraction arguments and arguments about later institutional use; it does not independently establish current restitution obligations or legal claims.
- Bandung as the first meeting of formerly ruled people as hosts is rhetorical, omitting earlier anticolonial/interstate conferences. Regional language categories, English as neutral for everyone, and broad commodity-export dependence need case-specific qualifications. Kipling's supplied English line is preserved verbatim; other historical quotations remain source paraphrases.

## Chapter 089: Race

- The pencil-test narrative compresses complex apartheid classification and appeal practices into a mechanical rule. The Pretoria family is explicitly a composite, not a documented named case. Historical racial labels and racist assertions are preserved as objects of the chapter's criticism, not endorsed descriptions.
- The Brazilian 1976 self-description study is called a census; the survey type needs checking. Numerous color categories do not establish weak racial hierarchy in Brazil. US one-drop classification and colonial laws inheriting enslaved status through the mother are distinct mechanisms, compressed together in the source.
- Lewontin's approximately 85-percent within-population partition does not establish the chapter's stronger claim about any arbitrarily chosen village pair versus continental averages. Genomic similarity percentages depend on the variants and comparison metric counted. Ancestry inference and rejection of racial ranking are distinct questions.
- The account of Morton presents disputed claims about measurement/data-selection bias as settled by later review; subsequent reanalyses and the distinction between measurement reliability and invalid racial inference require consideration. Linnaeus and Blumenbach's chronology, categories, and attitudes are compressed into a simplified scientific-history account.
- The maize analogy assumes sufficiently controlled within-field environments and comparable seed samples; merely planting seeds in one field does not make all within-field variation genetic. The example's central within-group/between-group distinction does not establish the causes of every observed human group difference.
- Tuskegee's forty-year history requires distinctions among withheld diagnosis, available treatment, and the post-penicillin period. The eGFR passage's placement of statistical creatinine differences in the biological layer does not itself establish genetic causation. These are translated historical/analytical examples, not medical guidance.
- Rwandan identities, colonial administration, and the 1994 genocide involved more than an identity-card classification mechanism; caste also has a varied history, not an unchanged two-thousand-year operation. Currency is not constituted solely by belief without legal/material institutions. Quotations are rendered from the supplied Chinese rather than authenticated English editions.

## Chapter 090: Women in History

- The Yorkshire census household is explicitly fictional and institutionally reconstructed. The blanket claim that 1891 census forms recorded wives' work as none needs checking against enumerator instructions and treatment of laundresses/lodging-house work. Tom is described as returning from a night shift, then again arriving home from work that evening. Soap-pod water is an unusual English-household detail; the translation retains its functional meaning as soapy water.
- Genealogies vary by period, region, class, and edition; the general claims that women's names, dates, and biographies were never recorded are rhetorical. Counting a great-grandmother as four generations includes the reader's own generation. Public/private-sphere categories are analytical models, not universal historical boundaries.
- Ancient Egyptian, Babylonian, Athenian and Homeric comparisons compress long periods and differing classes. Fatima al-Fihri's founding narrative and retrospective university status of al-Qarawiyyin require qualification. The origin of gender inequality and interpretations of Engels, Ban Zhao, Beauvoir and Scott are simplified frameworks, not settled causal findings.
- The source says French women first voted in 1944; enfranchisement was in 1944 and first nationwide voting followed in 1945. New Zealand's first-country description and UK eligibility need jurisdiction/qualification distinctions. The PRC Marriage Law's designation as the first law depends on the legislative category counted.
- The later reference to Black women relegated to the back of a Seneca Falls procession appears to conflate 1848 with later suffrage marches, notably 1913. The women's-movement wave model is regionally uneven. Wartime work and postwar suffrage had multiple causes; Britain enfranchised some women during 1918 before the armistice, not simply afterward.
- GDP is not limited strictly to market transactions: government nonmarket production, imputed housing services and certain own-account production are included. Household-care valuation, time-use ratios, accident statistics, and textbook counts require dates, denominators and sources not supplied. Counting care, paying wages for care, and divorce/property protection are distinct policy questions.

## Chapter 091: First World War

- Gift-box contents and distribution varied by recipient and date; the number distributed before Christmas differs from the eventual total. The truce scene combines reports from multiple sectors, with uncertain football details explicitly acknowledged. Broad claims about every soldier's wishes or everybody expecting a short war exceed the evidence presented.
- Trenches were not literally immobile for all four years. Verdun's deliberate bleed-France rationale and its documentary basis are debated. Casualty totals require distinguishing deaths, wounded, missing, military/civilian deaths, and geographic coverage. The translation preserves the source's approximate totals.
- The July Crisis account simplifies Princip's organizational connections, Serbian-government responsibility, French/Russian alliance obligations, British Entente commitments and Germany's operational planning. Italy's nonparticipation alongside the Central Powers in 1914 is omitted. Mobilization constraints were serious but not literally devoid of political choice or reversibility.
- Describing earlier wars as only armies against armies and this as the first large-scale state propaganda is overly categorical. Lusitania's roughly 1,200 dead included crew as well as passengers. Actual German atrocities and fabricated propaganda stories need distinguishing. Colonial-service totals combine different combatant/labor categories.
- Article 231 names Germany and its allies, rather than Germany alone, and established liability for specified Allied losses; its legal function, the political label war guilt, and an exhaustive historical-causation judgment are different. The source's claim that Versailles caused later mobilization is an interpretation, not a single-cause account of Nazism or the Second World War.
- Sykes–Picot did not directly draw all final mandates or modern Middle Eastern borders. The Shandong settlement transferred former German rights, not ownership of the entire province. Ho Chi Minh was already in France rather than simply traveling there for the petition; League enforcement included more than voluntary goodwill, despite major weaknesses.
- The discussion overstates Clark's thesis as nobody wanting general war and risks making systemic escalation inevitable. Nuclear arsenals cannot literally destroy the Earth as a planet dozens of times. The Washington–Moscow hotline was established after the Cuban Missile Crisis; wartime/crisis channels and the later hotline should not be conflated. US–Soviet direct/proxy clashes also complicate the categorical no-firing claim.
- Modern missile, cyberattack and warning-time statements depend on distance, systems and scenarios. The closing comparison compresses Russian collapse in 1917 and German mutiny in 1918. These are historical illustrations, not present-day military or legal instructions regarding orders.

## Chapter 092: Totalitarian Politics

- The opening combines a walnut-cased radio with the People's Receiver, whose best-known inexpensive model used a molded case; direct subsidies, prices, household coverage and radio ownership are simplified. Radio also entailed purchase/licensing costs. The bakery scene is not attached to a named archival case; its later same-street Kristallnacht description is narrative reconstruction.
- The Enabling Act was passed amid intimidation, exclusions and repression, not a free democratic procedure simply ending itself. Wannsee coordinated a genocide already in progress, rather than originating it. Great Purge executions and camp-admission/population totals need distinct date ranges. Famine causation, Nazi racial policy, Fascist Italy's own racism and Stalinist national/ethnic targeting require more detail than the three-label comparison permits.
- The White Rose already existed before February 1943. Eichmann's deportation role was more than neutral railway scheduling, as the chapter later acknowledges. Arendt's supposed initial expectation, diagnosis, reception and the claimed scholarly consensus about most participants' motives are contested. The assertion that every small refusal can be required is the author's ethical conclusion, not an empirical finding about equal risk.
- Milgram's two-thirds result belongs to a particular experimental condition, not all variants or humanity generally. Participant belief, recorded/live learner behavior, prods, replication limits and later interpretations need distinctions. This is a historical discussion, not an invitation to reproduce coercive/deceptive experiments.
- Bulgaria's protection of Jews within prewar borders must be distinguished from its authorities' deportation of Jews from occupied territories. The Protocols' forgery and dissemination are clear, but precise authorship/secret-police involvement is more contested than the source suggests. Religious and racial antisemitism did not simply replace one another.
- The United States lacked one-party dictatorship but the categorical no-secret-police phrasing omits investigative surveillance and state repression. Online-platform comparisons are structural analogies, not equivalence to genocide. Nazi, Rwandan and Armenian victim totals and groups require source-specific scope.
- Nuremberg did not state that superior orders can never affect mitigation: lack of complete exoneration, availability of moral choice, and mitigation are distinct. The translation preserves the chapter's simplified ethical/legal formulation, not current legal guidance.

## Chapter 093: Second World War

- The Spam description mixes container-opening mechanisms and generalizes original price positioning and distribution. The Chongqing shelter scene uses fictionalized characters; June 5 casualty uncertainty is explicitly retained. Multiple starting dates illuminate perspective but do not make all periodizations equally conventional or erase earlier Soviet involvement in the European conflict.
- June is described as part of the second half of 1941. Allied/Axis operational systems were not wholly unrelated before 1941; economic interdependence made expansion possible and attractive but not logically inevitable. Countries could remain militarily neutral despite economic involvement.
- Indian volunteer service, Bose's organization of the INA, Imphal's precise geography, African troops' removal and Paris liberation units are compressed. Thiaroye is introduced as after the war although December 1944 preceded war's end, an internal chronological defect. Javanese forced-labor totals across projects should not be identified wholesale with the Burma Railway.
- The Atlantic Charter was a joint declaration, not conventionally a signed treaty. The Cairo wording about territories taken before 1914 conflicts with its northeastern-China reference and the source's own account. Most importantly, British India was represented at San Francisco and was an original UN member, contrary to the claim that Indian representatives were absent. The source's passages on wartime promises remain unchanged in meaning.
- Oil import shares versus American shares of supply differ. Embargo and asset-freeze timing, Japan's options, German fuel requirements, the Hump routes and losses, and Burma Road length/time totals need more precise scope. The resource chain is an explanatory model, not proof of inevitability.
- War, Nanjing, genocide, forced-labor, bombing and atomic-death estimates use different periods and categories. The atomic-bomb invasion-casualty estimates and relative influence of surrender diplomacy, Soviet entry, signaling and institutional momentum are debated. Comparative explanations do not establish moral or legal equivalence, as the source explicitly states.
- National-memory sketches are broad authorial generalizations, not exhaustive accounts of citizens' views. Current UN, nuclear-arsenal, treaty, commemoration and judicial statements need date-specific verification for factual updating; translation preserves the supplied edition. Crimes against humanity terminology and limitations on orders/sovereignty have pre-1945 antecedents and later legal nuances.

## Chapter 094: Decolonization

- The initial coin description appears problematic: George VI coin portraits normally face left, but his Indian issues' legends and the 1950 coin's composition/dimensions require denomination-specific examination. George VI visited India before becoming king, contrary to the claim he never set foot there. Ashoka's capital and the modern coin emblem are not identical depictions of a full lotus-based monument.
- Radcliffe chaired two boundary commissions; lines were not a lone lawyer's unassisted act. Violence and migration began before final boundary publication, and imperial withdrawal, local political mobilization and organized violence contributed. Mountbatten advanced a deadline from June 1948, not simply several additional years. Gandhi's Calcutta location and fasting dates need separating.
- Ghana's first sub-Saharan independence claim needs qualifications for Sudan, Liberia, Ethiopia and the type of colonial transition meant. Congo's 1960 instability is anachronistic as an example used by pre-1957 Ghana independence opponents. The Berlin conference was in 1884–1885, about 75 years rather than half a century before 1960, and did not draw most African borders itself.
- US and Soviet positions on colonialism were inconsistent and strategic; neither universally opposed old empires, and the USSR's own domination requires differentiation. Suez included Israel as well as Britain and France. Angola's competing movements fought before formal 1975 independence, not only afterward.
- Cocoa/peanut/copper export patterns, railway functions and CFA monetary arrangements are generalized and date-dependent. West and Central African CFA systems and reform stages differ. Commodity terms-of-trade claims and Prebisch, dependency and world-systems theories are not identical or uncontested models.
- The source calls Edward Said a French-descended scholar; he was Palestinian American. This factual error is retained faithfully in the translation and flagged here. English is not India's only higher-education language; the railway network is not simply unchanged since independence. The concluding India–Pakistan frontier discussion also compresses boundaries, contested Kashmir and their different origins.
- Museum restitution, monetary reform and official-language policy passages reflect the source's time of writing, not independently updated current policy. Quotations remain renderings of the supplied Chinese rather than authenticated English transcripts.

## Chapter 095: Cold War

- The opening conflates the US fallout-shelter sign with the international radiation trefoil: they are different symbols. Radiation is not perceptible by human senses, but fallout consists of particles and is not literally always colorless/invisible. Shelter stockpiles, placement dates and cost claims need scope. This is historical description, not current emergency advice.
- B-59's crew numbers, exact authorization rules, Arkhipov's position, chronology and the verbal exchange derive from retrospective accounts with disputed details. The torpedo's yield was not exactly Hiroshima's. The one-person-saved-everyone narrative is a counterfactual interpretation, not a measured certainty.
- The categorical claim that US and Soviet servicemen never exchanged fire ignores covert direct encounters, including Soviet pilots in Korea. Eastern-bloc repression, proxy wars and Soviet–Chinese fighting complicate long-peace framing. Death totals differ by definitions, especially Afghanistan. The Korean armistice versus peace-treaty distinction should not be read as a comprehensive current legal opinion.
- Petrov did not control nuclear launch; warning reports passed through additional command and verification stages, so a report did not almost inevitably produce retaliation. Able Archer's danger and Soviet interpretation are debated. NORAD false-alarm incidents differed in mechanism. Schelling's risk manipulation is more nuanced than simply acting irrationally.
- The text names Cuba among examples of confrontation centered in Europe, a geographical inconsistency. Three documents appear under a heading referring to two declarations. Western and Soviet ideological, security and economic objectives were variable, as the source partly acknowledges.
- Today's nuclear totals and threats are source-era claims, not a new current count. Not every nuclear warhead is deployed in a silo or submarine. State space programs have not simply been replaced by commercial companies. Deterrence effectiveness and the degree of accident-driven risk remain contested explanations.

## Chapter 096: Beauty and the Beholder

- The gallery characters and geologist's exhaustive report are illustrative constructions. Claims of nearly universal sunset response and strong facial-symmetry/consonance agreement are asserted without studies and need qualification. Infant consonance findings, landscape-preference evolution and sunset/fire hypotheses are not settled universal explanations.
- Pythagoras's smithy story is legend; hammer-weight relations do not establish the string-length result in the way the anecdote implies. Hume's own standard-of-taste account goes beyond the subjectivist position assigned here. Cultural contrasts involving Song literati, European courts, wabi-sabi, blackened teeth and Rococo are imaginative generalizations.
- The source contains an apparent authoring artifact, the English word brief, in the Kant section. It is retained in English rather than silently deleted. Kant's disinterestedness and common sense are not simply instrumental indifference or identical sensory hardware, and the small/soft beautiful description is closer to a simplified Burkean characterization. Catharsis as emotional drainage is one disputed interpretation.
- Bodily self-reports are not literally incapable of error or self-misinterpretation. The four-statement tool distinguishes useful speech functions, not infallible epistemic categories. Zhuangzi's animals demonstrate a perspective argument, not empirical proof of an aesthetic metaphysics; the source's inference that beauty cannot exist without viewers remains philosophical.
- The beauty-not-pleasure chain is a rhetorical distinction and sits uneasily beside the chapter's own account of aesthetic pleasure. The Unicode not-equal signs are visible prose inherited from the source, not altered equations. The claims that every Guernica viewer felt discomfort and no one practices foot-binding today are categorical overstatements.
- Foot-binding, corsetry and cosmetic-surgery references critique harmful standards rather than instruct readers to perform them. The weight threshold translates Chinese one hundred jin as fifty kilograms. Aesthetic exposure effects vary with context and excessive repetition; recommendation mechanisms and the no-cost universal access claim are simplified.

## Chapter 097: Tragedy, Ruins and Storms

- The Athenian theater scene is a composite, not a cited eyewitness record. Fifth-century stone seating, audience composition, admissions and competition chronology require period-specific qualification. The postcard sales comparison and audience enjoyment claims lack figures. Source table columns and every row are preserved; the English table may require root layout wrapping to fit unchanged column styles.
- The tragic paradox's first premise says always, which is stronger than ordinary pain avoidance and makes a contradiction easier to generate. Plato's education objections and Aristotle's catharsis are simplified; catharsis as accumulated emotion draining is disputed and not established mental-health guidance.
- Burke's distinction between pain, terror and delight is more complex than danger being purely imagined. Roman spectators could be physically safe while witnessing real harm, so the arena challenges fiction-only accounts but not every safety-distance account. Kant's mathematical and dynamical sublime are condensed, with finite landscapes loosely described as unbounded.
- Cultural concepts such as mono no aware and karuna rasa do not establish a single universal recipe. The beautiful/sublime/grotesque/horror table is an authorial teaching framework, not mutually exclusive natural categories or rankings.
- Chernobyl tourism is described as a present popular activity from a source-era perspective; access and security changed substantially after 2022. This is not a travel recommendation or safety assurance. Storms, fire and disaster sites remain dangerous despite subjective feelings of distance; the chapter explicitly frames observations as safely separated rather than directing readers to approach.
- The assertion that eighteenth-century thinkers lacked the problem of consuming real suffering is inconsistent with older public executions/arenas and earlier aesthetic debate. Fiction does not guarantee that no participant is harmed in production, and documentary viewing can also have aesthetic dimensions. The ethical questions remain open authorial prompts, not universal rules that documentary equals voyeurism.

## Chapter 098: Art, Imitation and Creation

- The Athenian performance is reconstructed, including Plato's presence, spectators' later reactions, prisoners' release, masks and footwear. Oedipus's first-performance date and Plato's age require differentiation; raised tragic boots and monumental stone seating should not simply be projected into every fifth-century production. Artistic self-injury is described as fictional plot, not an invitation to imitate it.
- The Zeuxis, Parrhasius and Cao Buxing accounts are expressly legends. Plato's three beds are three levels, commonly described as imitation removed twice from the Form, not necessarily three separate intervals. Realism-as-neutral-recording is a teaching foil, not an exhaustive statement of Courbet or photographic theory.
- The source says Liu Xie came over eight hundred years after Gu Kaizhi, despite naming both with dates placing them around the fourth–sixth centuries; the interval is a clear chronological inconsistency retained. Liu's thought is related analogically to expression theory, not identical with Tolstoy. Song painting anecdotes and European medieval comparisons need genre- and period-specific qualification.
- African mask forms and meanings differ greatly among societies. Not every enlarged forehead signifies wisdom or every mask ancestral identity. Islamic religious aniconism has varied contexts and does not imply the absence of representational art across Islamic societies. Modernism's development is not reducible to one encounter with appropriated masks.
- Lascaux/Chauvet dates and cave locations are source-era generalizations; Altamira's cave and its paintings have different discovery dates. Not all painted caves were deep, uninhabited or inaccessible. Hypoxia/shamanic interpretations are hypotheses, not invitations to enter hazardous caves or empirical confirmation of specific ancient beliefs.
- Fountain's authorship, submission circumstances, magazine defense and disappearance have scholarly disputes; Danto encountered Warhol at a gallery, and Brillo packaging was for soap pads, not simply boxes of soap. Danto's artworld and Dickie's institutional theories differ and do not necessarily exclude every child's or outsider's art as asserted.
- Photography's supposed declaration of painting's death is disputed in attribution. Photography did not mechanically cause all subsequent art movements. Present AI performance, expression and intention are source-era claims and philosophical questions, not independently tested capability guarantees.

## Chapter 099: Fountain and Art Institutions

- The 1917 opening's venue and hanging method, exact submission origin, original purchase/manufacturer, Duchamp's authorship and alias origin require evidence-specific qualification. Duchamp was 29 in April 1917, not yet 30. The first Fountain's position and the location of its visible signature are awkwardly described as downward-facing, despite the photographic view. Stieglitz's photograph does not simply show it lying on the floor.
- The source's claims of perfectly calm resignation without statement and no uncertainty in who submitted the work exceed the surviving record. The 1964 authorized edition is distinct from other authorized replicas. Contemporary gallery availability and price comparisons are not present-day visitor or purchasing advice.
- Scholar's rocks, Ido bowls and West African ritual objects are useful comparisons but their histories, functions, crafting and acquisition differ. Ido bowls' characterization as simply Korean peasant rice bowls is debated; natural stones can also be shaped or worked. Selection practices do not make all traditions equivalent to Duchamp's institutional challenge.
- The aesthetic-relocation explanation cites Duchamp's visual indifference as corroboration despite its tension with selection for beauty. The source rightly treats the antiwar/Dada interpretation as an interpretation, not settled personal intent. The claim that the first ordinary object entered art in 1917 conflicts with both older examples and Duchamp's earlier readymades.
- Brillo boxes packaged soap pads, not laundry powder. Warhol's 1964 gallery display and the comparison with supermarket cartons should not be taken as proof that original commercial cartons were physically intermixed exactly as narrated. Danto and Dickie have distinct, evolving theories; actual formulations and outsider implications are more complex than a museum automatically conferring status.
- Van Meegeren's investigation, supervised demonstration, trial and the identity of the work sold to Göring are compressed. A provenance change can justifiably alter historical evaluation without proving all judgments by deceived experts sound. Museum objects can be historical exhibits without becoming fine-art works.
- Comedian's 2019 prices are explicitly historical and vary by edition; certificates, installation rights, resale and ownership are legally distinct matters not independently advised here. The AI-authorship question is normative and aesthetic, not a current copyright-law determination.

## Chapter 100: Musical Meaning

- The opening describes a familiar song yet then says no words/concepts appear, apparently shifting to instrumental sound without making that transition explicit. The classroom is an illustrative composite. Assertions that nobody hears funeral music as celebration and that sound/emotion mappings are universal conflict with the later cultural qualifications and jazz-funeral example.
- Crying and laughter do not invariably have the specified pitch/tempo directions. Major/minor affect, infant recognition, embodied response, prediction and memory involve variable cultural and experimental evidence. Memories do not demonstrably bypass prediction simply because a listener reports association.
- Equal temperament divides an octave equally in logarithmic pitch/ratios, not equal frequency differences. Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier does not establish use of modern twelve-tone equal temperament; well temperaments and equal temperament differ. Gamelan tuning is diverse, not one alternative scale. The rhythm example's waltz/heartbeat distinction and natural harmonic-resolution language are simplified.
- The five-layer framework is useful but not universally equally applicable: some traditions lack Western-style functional harmony, and classifying the Islamic call to prayer as music may conflict with practitioners' categories. Drum languages and Dong grand-song organization also vary and should not be flattened into one model.
- Dates, authorship and interpretation of the Record of Music and Great Preface are complex. Confucius's three-month response is a transmitted textual account, not a independently verified physiological fact. Quotations, including the Farewell lyric, are translated from supplied Chinese rather than borrowed from a published modern English translation.
- Hanslick's argument about determinate emotions and formal content is simplified; emotions without specific objects and his relation to affect remain debated. Birthday melody origins and Wagner's Bridal Chorus versus other wedding marches need distinctions. Anthems can have acoustically identifiable differences even if anthem status depends on convention.
- Classical and folk categories predate parts of the concert/exam/recording infrastructure identified here; Bach was not exclusively a church employee throughout his career. Early commercial record formats generally involved shellac discs, not simply wax records. Present song durations and recommendation-system claims are date/platform-dependent generalizations, not tested descriptions of every service.
- The account of disappearing Dong practice is a hypothetical preservation question, not an independently verified report that the tradition or its final groups have already vanished. Audio recordings retain a representation of sound, not perfectly identical original sound waves or the entire lived event.
