Pyramid labor not enslaved does not mean wholly voluntary, as the source itself cautions.
- `Two Plans`: the sentence literally says heaven is not round but earth is square, unlike the familiar round-heaven/square-earth formulation; retained. The Kaogong ji quote is newly translated from supplied Chinese, not copied from a modern published translation; dating and influence are schematic. Li and track units vary historically. Roman concrete spans ten times larger is an unsourced generalization.
- `Cathedral height`: Beauvais choir vault collapse and later crossing-spire collapse are distinct events, not necessarily the same vault failing twice during construction. - `Default citizen`: source first says design can lie, then concludes architecture never lies, a rhetorical tension retained. Accessibility cost and default-user majority claims are not universal cost-benefit findings; curb-ramp histories predate some 1970s campaigns. All ten source sections and the classical quotation remain, without layout or structural changes.
