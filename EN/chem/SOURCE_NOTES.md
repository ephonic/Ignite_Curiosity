# Potential inconsistencies in the Chinese source

These notes identify potential inconsistencies encountered while translating. They are not a comprehensive independent fact-check. The English translation retains the source's scientific claims, numerical expressions, formulas, and chapter references. Health and safety concerns below are source claims requiring review, not recommendations from the translator. Section names reference the original files under `chem/latex/chapters/`.

## Chapter 1 — ch01.tex

- The quantitative bridge after “化学家的三种语言” calculates approximately 1.7 × 10²² glucose molecules, but later spells out 一万亿亿 (10²⁰). The comparison following two trillion molecules per person with all terrestrial sand grains also appears inconsistent.
- The molecular definition of physical/chemical changes is deliberately simplified and does not cover all types of matter; the chapter itself discusses some limits.

## Chapter 2 — ch02.tex

- “把测量写成符号” describes positive/negative scientific-notation exponents using apparently reversed decimal-position language.
- “回到那瓶花露水” says alternative explanations *except* psychological suggestion have been excluded, apparently contrary to the intended evidential conclusion.
- The helium discovery account says more than ninety million kilometers from Earth, apparently mixing the familiar Sun-distance figure in miles with kilometers.
- The cold-fusion, Brownian-motion, cathode-ray, and sensory-detection historical accounts contain simplifications that may merit checking.

## Chapter 3 — ch03.tex

- “现在，请符号登场” lists H₂O, O₂, CO₂, then calls all of them compounds before correctly identifying oxygen as elemental.
- The purity bridge equates 10²⁰ impurity particles with 一亿亿个的一百倍 (10¹⁸).
- “纯的边界” gives one deuterium per 3200 hydrogen atoms and refers to trace isotopologues collectively as heavy-water molecules; the numerical abundance and terminology need review.
- Exercise 2 requests at least three particle types in both ordinary sugar water and soda.
- Fixed melting/boiling points and homogeneous-alloy statements are broad simplifications.

## Chapter 4 — ch04.tex

- **Health/safety — source review needed:** “六个名字，一条规律” presents diluted alcohol skin rubbing for fever as an ordinary cooling example.
- “三态之外的世界” classifies refrigerator jelly as glassy material and assumes the reader's phone display uses liquid crystals.
- “回到那袋消失的冰” gives approximately 79°C as the dry-ice/skin temperature difference.
- “从冻干咖啡到恒星” lists liquid nitrogen for welding among liquid-gas uses.

## Chapter 5 — ch05.tex

- “原子小到什么程度” asserts that individual atoms can never be weighed. It also gives inconsistent spelled-out counts and counting durations: 7 × 10²⁴ versus 七千万亿亿 (7 × 10²³), and thirty thousand years for all humanity counting one molecule per second.
- “碳原子当秤砣” spells a mole as 6020 亿亿亿 (6.02 × 10²⁷), inconsistent with the displayed Avogadro value.
- The bridge after “摩尔像打” says 10¹⁰ moles exceeds all the world's water.
- Statements of exact equality between molar mass in grams and relative mass should be read alongside the chapter's 2019 redefinition discussion.
- **Health — source review needed:** Concentration examples define physiological saline's 0.9% as mass per 100 grams before using the customary grams-per-liter value; blood-glucose wording is simplified. The cola/sugar-cube and later blood-glucose/sugar-cube comparisons imply differing cube sizes.

## Chapter 6 — ch06.tex

- The quantitative bridge spells 1.7 × 10²¹ as 一千七百亿亿 (1.7 × 10¹⁹), while “17 followed by 20 zeros” agrees with the displayed value. Its drop-versus-global-cup comparison also needs checking.
- “在家观察分子的撞击” attributes visible ink tendrils directly to molecular impacts, overlooking macroscopic fluid motion.
- “原子有多小，核有多空” and the challenge use a geometric direct-hit nuclear-area model for Rutherford scattering; the main text and challenge give different orders of magnitude, and electrostatic deflection is substantially simplified.
- Several references point forward to Chapter 4 despite appearing in Chapter 6.

## Chapter 7 — ch07.tex

- “质子数” describes Moseley as bombarding elements with X-rays rather than distinguishing excitation from emitted characteristic X-rays.
- “给原子称体重” generalizes airport trace detectors as miniature mass spectrometers.
- The chlorine average bridge attributes the 35.5-versus-35.45 difference solely to exact isotope masses, overlooking the rounded 75%/25% abundances.
- The carbon-14 estimate spells ordinary carbon as 六十万亿亿亿 (6 × 10²⁹), inconsistent with its calculated 6 × 10²⁶.
- **Health — source review needed:** Breath-test suitability and isotope safety are presented broadly, without individual clinical qualifications.

## Chapter 8 — ch08.tex

- The iodine-131 bridge calculates 80 days, then describes disappearance after “a few weeks.”
- **Health/safety — source review needed:** “放射性、剂量与风险” calls injury above 1000 mSv in a short interval certain and serious and describes low natural internal activity as completely harmless.
- **Health/safety — source review needed:** The misconception box broadly says radiotherapy patients do not become radioactive, without separating external beams from internal radionuclide treatment/brachytherapy. It also overgeneralizes activation as requiring reactor-level neutron conditions.
- **Safety — source review needed:** The smoke-alarm explanation generalizes long-lived source output into decades without maintenance; the source's explicit prohibition on dismantling is preserved.
- Decay independence from external conditions, stability of small nuclei, iron as the most tightly bound nucleus, and zero carbon emissions for nuclear power are simplified statements.
- The beta-decay equation omits the antineutrino described in the accompanying prose; the equation is preserved.

## Chapter 9 — ch09.tex

- Opening and conclusion say sodium can emit only the yellow color and that changing elements is the only means of changing a lamp's color.
- Rutherford's gold-foil discussion is attributed to Chapter 7 instead of Chapter 6.
- The photon bridge spells 3 × 10²⁰ as 三十万亿亿 (3 × 10²¹).
- The conclusion calls 589 nm the wavelength of greatest human visual sensitivity.
- Electron-position, trajectory, single-particle-cloud, and Bell-test statements simplify distinctions between measurement and quantum interpretations.
- The flame exercise's description of an ordinary candle as mostly pale blue may not match the proposed observation.

## Chapter 10 — ch10.tex

- Mendeleev's story says “sixteen years later, in 1875” after 1869.
- Pool disinfectant is generalized as molecular Cl₂.
- Shell capacities 2n² and period lengths are conflated in the challenge.
- Noble-gas nonbonding and same-column equal outer-electron counts are broad introductory generalizations.

## Chapter 11 — ch11.tex

- Electron-affinity prose compares “chloride ion plus free electron” with chlorine atom plus electron, apparently retaining an extra electron on the product side.
- The radius table uses Na = 166 pm while later examples use 186 pm; the chapter discusses measurement conventions but does not explicitly reconcile this switch.
- Cesium is called the element with the lowest ionization energy.
- “烧开约5°C” and the final exercise use boiling language for a temperature increase; English renders the intended heating-by-degrees operation.
- The effective-charge model is explicitly rough; same-shell shielding and equality down groups should not be interpreted as precise statements.

## Chapter 12 — ch12.tex

- Aluminum is called more reactive than magnesium.
- **Safety/quantity — source review needed:** The sodium-energy example describes a 2.3 g demonstration piece as rice-sized and says it heats half a cup nearly to boiling; the numerical calculation, size description, and water quantity are inconsistent. The explicit ban on attempting the metal/water demonstrations remains in translation.
- Davy's historical account identifies soda/potash as carbonates and describes melting potash, whereas the actual experimental substances and procedure need checking; his institutional affiliation is also simplified.
- The beryllium discussion asks why the top of Group 2 should be most reactive, conflicting with the stated downward trend.
- **Health — source review needed:** Calcium-tablet, barium-meal, and emergency-treatment exercise wording are broad and should undergo medical review. Existing directions to follow a doctor and discuss emergency principles only are preserved.

## Chapter 13 — ch13.tex

- “一勺盐” calls 5 × 10²² divided by 8 × 10⁹ about sixty trillion rather than roughly six trillion.
- The final preview says nitrogen has three atoms joined by shared electrons rather than two atoms with a triple bond.
- The Na₂He example gives 2016 and claims pressures above Earth's center; both should be checked.
- Bartlett's product composition is represented as XeF⁺ with PtF₅⁻; the historical material's actual speciation is more complicated and the listed approximation merits review.
- **Health/safety — source review needed:** Chloride and fluoride are described as harmless after electron gain, and a faint chlorine smell is treated as evidence of a safe disinfection window. These are source generalizations, not independent safety endorsements.
- Exercise 5 first refers to an argon balloon, then asks about helium versus hydrogen; both are retained.

## Chapter 14 — ch14.tex

- Haber's 1909 apparatus is described as using an iron catalyst; early experimental catalyst history merits checking.
- The nitrogen bridge equates 15 kg nitrogen with nitrogen in 90 kg ammonium nitrate, inconsistent with the salt's composition.
- References to carbon in Chapter 36 and respiration in Chapter 54 may not match the book's chapter layout.
- **Health — source review needed:** Classroom stuffiness is predominantly attributed to blood carbon-dioxide accumulation, and theoretical oxygen availability is presented without usable-concentration limits.
- The sulfuric-acid production account directly absorbs SO₃ in water; industrial process details are simplified.
- **Health/safety:** Existing source warnings about hydrogen sulfide's loss of smell, professional handling, and harmful concentrated ozone are retained.

## Chapter 15 — ch15.tex

- Metallic copper is described as having a nearly, but not completely, filled 3d shell, and metallic colors as simple d-to-d transitions.
- The iron calculation displays 4 × 10²² but spells 四万亿亿 (4 × 10²⁰); all adult-body iron is then rhetorically assigned oxygen transport.
- Hemoglobin is described as containing one central iron complex per molecule rather than distinguishing its subunits/hemes.
- **Health — source review needed:** Vitamin B12 supply is attributed to intestinal bacteria and food without qualification about human absorption of gut-produced B12.
- The closing challenge claims tenfold smaller splitting would make every complex black rather than accounting for infrared absorption.
- Zinc/mercury classification, universal transition-metal colors, ferromagnetism explanations, and rust controls are introductory simplifications. The oil-covered cup uses unboiled tap water and therefore does not eliminate initially dissolved oxygen.
- The catalyst mountain metaphor contains internally awkward wording about impossible crossings versus changing the barrier; the intended thermodynamics/kinetics distinction is retained without changing the source claim.

## Chapter 16 — ch16.tex

- “先盘点”: the inventory is introduced as mass percentages, but the atmospheric figures are volume/mole percentages; carbon dioxide is listed in an element inventory. The five-column table may require layout review in English.
- “那原子核里的元素”: the three-factory account says all elements heavier than iron require violent stellar deaths/collisions, omitting slow neutron capture in stars. The qualitative stellar fuel timetable and abundance claims are retained.
- “用符号”: proton number is said to be conserved in nuclear reactions, and the simplified four-proton fusion equation omits charged products. The Hg-197 to Au-197 equation shows positron emission; the actual decay mode requires review. All source equations remain unchanged.
- The gold-ring equation gives 1.5 × 10²² atoms, but 一万五千亿亿 spells 1.5 × 10²⁰; the English preserves both quantities.
- “科学家凭什么知道”: the 1957 B2FH narrative appears to attribute a neutron-star-merger prediction to that paper; the 2017 event's several hundred Earth masses of gold also needs checking.
- “元素不出门”: phosphorus is categorically said to have no gaseous form. “这条规则在哪里会失灵” conflates tiny mass changes with atom conservation being approximate.
- **Experiment review:** Inflating a sealed bag changes buoyancy, so the claim that before/after scale readings differ only within instrumental uncertainty needs checking. The source warning against sealed glass vessels is retained.

## Chapter 17 — ch17.tex

- **Safety — source review needed:** The opening explicitly asks readers to strike a match, watch it for three seconds, and describes heat reaching the hand; appropriate supervised-demonstration warnings should be considered. The translation preserves the source directions rather than silently endorsing them.
- “一条曲线”: the per-bond equation divides joules by a constant expressed in mol⁻¹, producing a dimensional mismatch; the numerical result is retained. The match example displays 10²¹ but spells 一亿亿亿 (10²⁴).
- “能量从哪来”: the text says actual combustion releases more heat than the gas-phase-water estimate without specifying condensation conditions.
- “八隅体”: larger electron-shell “floors” are used to explain expanded octets; XePtF₆ is used as the unqualified historical product formula.
- “科学家怎样知道” and the challenge: Heitler/London are located in Germany, and bond formation is explained as lowering both electron kinetic and potential energy. These historical and quantum-mechanical claims, including the virial-theorem relation, merit review. Infrared vibration frequency alone is presented as an indirect bond-energy measurement.
- The Morse potential has a different energy-zero convention from the plotted curve. The misconception box treats macroscopic wire fracture and spontaneous water decomposition as direct demonstrations, simplifying the distinction between thermodynamics, mechanisms, and activation barriers.
- Chapter references for ATP (46), kinetics (28), and “Volume Three” need cross-checking. The next-chapter preview suggests salt itself is suitable crucible material.

## Chapter 18 — ch18.tex

- **Safety — source review needed:** “三杯水的导电测试” electrolyzes saltwater with graphite electrodes and encourages close observation of bubbles, attributing them solely to water decomposition. Chlorine/hypochlorite production and suitable ventilation/scale controls require review. Step (1) instructs briefly touching electrodes, while the safety paragraph prohibits their touching as a short-circuit risk. Both source statements are retained.
- The opening and “回到那颗盐粒” imply an ordinary stove flame cannot reach salt's melting point, conflating flame temperature, heat transfer, and lattice energy.
- “规则层”: the gas-phase ionization/affinity subtotal excludes the electrostatic binding of an isolated ion pair, then concludes ionic bonding occurs only in crystals; a later paragraph explicitly acknowledges gas-phase NaCl molecules. The −640 kJ/mol account starts from gaseous atoms, not the standard elemental reactants in the displayed reaction.
- “有的盐溶于水”: solubility is reduced to a balance of lattice and hydration energies, omitting entropy/free energy; readily soluble NaCl is treated as if hydration necessarily exceeds lattice separation energy.
- **Health — source review needed:** The barium-contrast paragraph generalizes safety from insolubility and says toxicity depends entirely on crystal form. Preserve as source explanation, not advice for ingesting materials or substituting nonmedical barium sulfate.
- The closing challenge uses inverse-square decay in the Madelung energy sum and interprets the Madelung constant as a factor in net attraction rather than electrostatic energy; the force/energy distinction needs review.

## Chapter 19 — ch19.tex

- **Safety — source review needed:** “当交出电子” says hydrogen can be stored quietly in air provided it is not ignited; this should not be read as safe storage guidance for flammable mixtures. The opening treats medical oxygen-cylinder color as universal although conventions vary.
- “给分子画结构图”: CO's actual dipole is said to leave oxygen slightly negative, contrary to the usual measured dipole direction. Formal-charge arithmetic is retained unchanged.
- The ozone explanation describes “two extra electron pairs” delocalizing to make 1.5-order bonds; its electron accounting needs review.
- The water-stream experiment treats deflection as proof of permanent molecular polarity and says a nonpolar stream would ignore the charged comb, omitting induced polarization and other electrostatic effects.
- “科学家怎样知道”: the 1927 Schrödinger equation is called two years old, conflicting with Chapter 17's year-after-publication narrative; Heitler–London numerical agreement is overstated. The elementary purely electrostatic sharing account and later quantum account need careful reconciliation.
- Several approximations differ from earlier tables (H–H 432 versus 436, nitrogen 945 versus 946; electronegativity differences do not all match the individual values quoted). The source also says sugar decomposes without a chance to melt.
- The challenge describes covalent bonding as invariably paired while later acknowledging oxygen's unpaired electrons; coordinate-bond bookkeeping and radical reactivity are simplified. Nitrate's exercise asks each oxygen's share of negative charge without distinguishing formal/resonance bookkeeping from real partial charge.

## Chapter 20 — ch20.tex

- Opening and “回到那颗橙子”: the orange/lemon contrast is attributed to opposite limonene enantiomers as the dominant oils; this familiar oversimplification needs review against actual citrus-oil composition. “香菜籽（葛缕子）” conflates coriander and caraway; both names remain visible in English.
- **Health — source review needed:** The thalidomide account divides sedative and teratogenic effects cleanly between mirror images, then correctly notes in-body interconversion. The division and broad present-day drug-approval/single-enantiomer statistics require medical/regulatory review, not reliance as medication advice.
- The heading promises four basic shapes but lists five. The water diagram's drawn bonds form a 90° schematic angle despite its 104.5° label; coordinates/styles are intentionally unchanged.
- The balloon procedure ties long balloons at their midpoints, potentially yielding a different arm count than the intended two/three/four-domain model; squeezing a balloon is not necessarily equivalent to increasing lone-pair repulsion.
- “都是极性键”: lack of a dipole is treated as nearly absent attraction and as sufficient to explain dry-ice sublimation, omitting pressure/phase conditions and dispersion. Shape-only accounts of smell, ultraviolet absorption, and function are broad simplifications.
- The claim that fragrance factories no longer extract citrus peel oils but synthesize chosen enantiomers, and categorical statements about glucose/protein size and atoms having no color, need review.
- “形状之后”: calls Chapter 22 the next chapter, skipping Chapter 21. Protein/benzene/organic-volume cross-references should be checked.

## Chapter 21 — ch21.tex

- **Safety — source review needed:** The opening suggests bending a glass rod by hand until it breaks, with no shard/eye-protection warning. The LED circuit has no required current-limiting resistor and treats a low-resistance metal test object as a resistor substitute; a 9 V supply can damage the LED. Exercise 3 discusses twisted/taped wire repair without explicitly restricting it to a disconnected conceptual example; exercise 4 asks skin contact with freezer-cold metal. Root should consider separate warnings.
- “一副模型” and the conclusion group glass with alternating ionic crystals and attribute its fracture to like-charge alignment; this does not correctly describe typical glass's network fracture.
- Drift-speed example equates 1 A with a small desk lamp without stating voltage and compares 0.07 mm/s to a snail without a specified speed. Near-light-speed signaling is described as simultaneous wire-wide motion, simplifying finite propagation.
- The sea/band account describes only roughly one percent of electrons as “truly free,” conflating thermal excitations, heat capacity, and current response. The $N$ example spells 一亿亿亿 (10²⁴); the final copper-wire paragraph spells 亿亿亿亿 (10³²), a very different scale. All source magnitudes remain translated literally.
- “合金”: pure gold is categorically called unusable for rings, alloys categorically not compounds, and bronze humanity's first alloy; these statements omit qualifications, intermetallic phases, and historical complexity. Shape-memory warm-water behavior depends on alloy treatment/transition temperature.
- Steel's stainless threshold is given as about 12% chromium and implies nickel is required; Chapter 15 is referred to as a future encounter despite preceding this chapter.

## Chapter 22 — ch22.tex

- “粘住、聚成球”: narrowing of a falling tap stream is grouped with surface-tension phenomena without noting gravitational acceleration and continuity. The water-strider example specifies four dimples.
- The model categorically excludes hydrogen bonds from chemical bonds and from intramolecular interactions; these are introductory conventions, not universal definitions. It also claims intermolecular attractions can never exceed covalent bonds.
- Boiling/evaporation comparisons use differing temperatures for latent heats; faster evaporation and cooling are attributed directly to lower latent heat, without vapor-pressure/rate qualifications.
- “给看不见”: real-gas pressure is said always to be below ideal predictions, overlooking repulsion at high density. The universal ordering of dispersion, dipole, and hydrogen-bond strengths is only approximate.
- **Health — source review needed:** Inhaled-anesthetic action is assigned to dissolving in nerve-cell lipid membranes; this simplified mechanism needs medical review. Repeated alcohol-on-skin cooling examples are source illustrations, not fever-care recommendations.
- The final preview says knowing only attraction would predict water boiling below methane, conflicting with the preceding explanation. Cling-film adhesion, molecular-size trends, and peeling all contacts “simultaneously” simplify real materials/mechanics.
- The Lennard–Jones challenge calls the r⁻⁶ potential-energy term the distance dependence of force/attraction; force has a different derivative exponent. The equation itself is preserved.

## Chapter 23 — ch23.tex

- **Health/safety — source review needed:** Opening and repeated passages present warm-water sponging as fever treatment, and the solvent paragraph explicitly suggests gasoline or alcohol for removing paint/engine oil from hands. These are source claims requiring separate professional safety review, not translator advice.
- “特别扛热”: heat is described as first breaking hydrogen bonds and only afterward increasing temperature; the sequential account is an oversimplification. All mammals are broadly described as maintaining about 37°C, and human temperature stability is primarily assigned to body water.
- “挑食”: ethanol is grouped with nonpolar solvents; oil/paint removal is generalized. NaCl dissolution is reduced to energetic compensation without entropy, and the hydrophobic-cage picture and universal hollow-sphere self-assembly omit scale/composition qualifications.
- The sunlight oil/water experiment uses equal volumes, potentially differing absorption and containers, so it does not isolate specific heat alone. The glass-thermometer warning is retained verbatim in meaning.
- “科学家怎么知道”: atomic imaging is categorically said impossible with any microscope; the ice/neutron/Pauling chronology and attribution of the hydrogen-bond concept need checking. Proton disorder persisting to absolute zero is presented without ice-ordering qualifications.
- “这张网也有边界”: denser high-pressure ice is described as something that could simply be dropped into ordinary water and sink, ignoring its stability conditions. Claims that all prior water treatments cease to matter on ingestion should distinguish transient structure from persistent solutes/contaminants.
- “回到那杯”: sea-ice melting is said not to change sea level, and land-ice entry is identified as the cause, omitting density/salinity nuance and thermal expansion.
- Exercise 1 asks for only three neighbors but four hydrogen-bond “hands.” Exercise 3 applies a 4°C lake stratification picture to a flowing river without qualification. Chapter 28's entropy and Chapter 51's membrane references need cross-checking.
