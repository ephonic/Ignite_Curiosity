# Potential inconsistencies in the Chinese source

These notes identify potential inconsistencies encountered while translating. They are not a comprehensive independent fact-check. The English translation retains the source's scientific claims, numerical expressions, formulas, and chapter references. Health and safety concerns below are source claims requiring review, not recommendations from the translator. Section names reference the original files under `chem/latex/chapters/`.

## Chapter 1 — ch01.tex

- The quantitative bridge after “化学家的三种语言” calculates approximately 1.7 × 10²² glucose molecules, but later spells out 一万亿亿 (10²⁰). The comparison following two trillion molecules per person with all terrestrial sand grains also appears inconsistent.
- The molecular definition of physical/chemical changes is deliberately simplified and does not cover all types of matter; the chapter itself discusses some limits.

## Chapter 2 — ch02.tex

- “把测量写成符号” describes positive/negative scientific-notation exponents using apparently reversed decimal-position language.
- “回到那瓶花露水” says alternative explanations *except* psychological suggestion have been excluded, apparently contrary to the intended evidential conclusion.
- The helium discovery account says more than ninety million kilometers from Earth, apparently mixing the familiar Sun-distance figure in miles with kilometers.
- The cold-fusion, Brownian-motion, cathode-ray, and sensory-detection historical accounts contain simplifications that may merit checking.

## Chapter 3 — ch03.tex

- “现在，请符号登场” lists H₂O, O₂, CO₂, then calls all of them compounds before correctly identifying oxygen as elemental.
- The purity bridge equates 10²⁰ impurity particles with 一亿亿个的一百倍 (10¹⁸).
- “纯的边界” gives one deuterium per 3200 hydrogen atoms and refers to trace isotopologues collectively as heavy-water molecules; the numerical abundance and terminology need review.
- Exercise 2 requests at least three particle types in both ordinary sugar water and soda.
- Fixed melting/boiling points and homogeneous-alloy statements are broad simplifications.

## Chapter 5 — ch05.tex

- “原子小到什么程度” asserts that individual atoms can never be weighed. It also gives inconsistent spelled-out counts and counting durations: 7 × 10²⁴ versus 七千万亿亿 (7 × 10²³), and thirty thousand years for all humanity counting one molecule per second.
- “碳原子当秤砣” spells a mole as 6020 亿亿亿 (6.02 × 10²⁷), inconsistent with the displayed Avogadro value.
- The bridge after “摩尔像打” says 10¹⁰ moles exceeds all the world's water.
- Statements of exact equality between molar mass in grams and relative mass should be read alongside the chapter's 2019 redefinition discussion.
- **Health — source review needed:** Concentration examples define physiological saline's 0.9% as mass per 100 grams before using the customary grams-per-liter value; blood-glucose wording is simplified. The cola/sugar-cube and later blood-glucose/sugar-cube comparisons imply differing cube sizes.
