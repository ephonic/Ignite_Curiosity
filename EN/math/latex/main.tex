\chapter*{Preface: Don't Rush to Memorize Formulas}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}
This book is a tour of mathematics for middle school students. It does not follow the textbook's order to check what you ``ought to know.'' Instead, it starts with real, intriguing questions and takes you along to see how mathematicians pose problems, invent concepts, look for evidence, and connect their ideas.

As you read, you can temporarily set aside standard answers and lists of formulas---but please keep two things: your curiosity and the habit of asking ``why?'' For every key conclusion, we offer an intuitive explanation, a necessary proof, or an experiment you can check for yourself.

The reflection questions at the end of each chapter have three levels: observation, proof, and challenge. We give hints, but no direct answers. Getting stuck is normal. Getting stuck is precisely where mathematics begins.
